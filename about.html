Established 2025

PT. Melodex Sonavia International

Founded in 2025, PT. Melodex Sonavia International (MELODEX) operates as a music publishing and creative content company within the IRCOMM Group, under its parent company PT. IRCOMM Norton Capital.

We specialize in the professional management of intellectual property, focusing on music rights administration, licensing, and strategic creative support. Our mission is to help creators and industry partners navigate the commercial and legal aspects of music with clarity and confidence.

MELODEX offers comprehensive, end-to-end solutions—ranging from copyright registration and rights management to licensing facilitation, royalty tracking and distribution, as well as creative project development. Our services support songwriters, recording artists, brands, and content producers across multiple platforms and industries.
